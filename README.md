**Salesforce Hotel Booking System**-

This Hotel Booking System built on Salesforce provides a complete solution for managing hotel operations, leveraging the full capabilities of the Salesforce platform. The project integrates custom objects, automation, reporting, and dashboards to streamline processes such as hotel and room management, guest tracking, parking logistics, and most notably, guest feedback and service improvement workflows. The system is designed with scalability in mind, making it ideal for single hotels or large chains. By using a combination of Salesforce tools like Salesforce Flow, Reports, Dashboards, Custom Objects, and Process Builder, the system automates critical tasks and offers deep insights into hotel performance.

**Key Features:**

1. **Custom Objects:**
  Hotel & Rooms: Manage hotels and their rooms, tracking availability and guest stays.
  Guest Stay & Vehicle Parking: Track which guest stayed in which room and whether they brought a vehicle requiring parking.

2. **Feedback Mechanism (Highlight):**
  A robust feedback system collects guest ratings on aspects like room quality and service. If feedback is below a threshold, a case is automatically created, allowing hotel     staff to address issues promptly. This feature took significant effort and is the standout part of the project.
  
3. **Dashboards:**
  Hotel Booking: Shows booking trends and occupancy.
  Parking Information: Tracks vehicle parking availability.
  Customer Satisfaction Index:
  Analyzes guest feedback and case resolution performance.

**Unique Value:**

  The feedback mechanism, which automatically raises cases based on guest ratings, sets this system apart. This automation ensures that negative feedback is acted upon           immediately, maintaining high guest satisfaction and service quality. The time and effort spent in perfecting this system underscore its importance, as it guarantees           continuous service improvement.

**Benefits:**

  This Salesforce-based solution offers a scalable and efficient way to manage all aspects of hotel operations. The combination of automation, custom reporting, and real-time    dashboards allows hotel staff to be proactive and data-driven, ensuring the best possible guest experience while streamlining back-end processes.
